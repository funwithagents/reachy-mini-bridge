# The users present, and the faces published on the set in view

**Status:** Done

Implements `specs/vision/user_perception.md` ("The users present", "The report is an observable", "The detection loop") and the `users` property of `specs/core/bridge.md`. The detection loop publishes `bridge.users` — the confirmed tracks it holds, kept through the robot's own moves, an arrival or a departure with its reason each a change — and `bridge.faces` publishes on the set of faces in view instead of their count. Builds on the observable's `every` (`plans/202611121444_observable-every-value.md`). Leaves out identity, the head tracker's choice rules and the personal space event, and the control panel.

## Scope

- `src/reachy_mini_bridge/face_detection.py` — `User`, `UserDeparture`, `UsersReport`, the confirmation constants; `_FaceTracks` recording each update's in-view verdicts and drops with their rule; `_Users`, the presence state; the loop publishing it, clearing the tracks (and the people as `"stopped"`) at a start, a stop, a detector swap and a detector down; `_publish` on the set of track ids
- `src/reachy_mini_bridge/bridge.py` — the `users` observable (`CHANGE_HISTORY`), handed to the loop, reset at exit; the `faces` docstring
- `src/reachy_mini_bridge/__init__.py` — the three types exported
- `tests/test_face_detection.py` — the presence state, the loop's publications, the faces' swap rule
- `tests/test_bridge.py` — `users` in and out of a session; the projecting emotion publishing nothing on `users`; the front door
- `tests-e2e/test_perception.py`, `tests-e2e/test_head_tracking.py` — an arrival and a departure on the viewer sim; an emotion away from a portrait publishing nothing on `users`
- Specs: `vision/user_perception.md`, `core/bridge.md` (Updated → Implemented), `core/observable.md`, `motion/head_tracking.md`, `_overview.md`, `_index.md`; docs: the API reference, the perception guide, getting started, the README

## Steps

1. Types and constants; `_FaceTracks.update` records `in_view` per held track and `dropped` as `(id, "left" | "unseen")` by the rule that fired.
2. `_Users.observe(report, tracks)`: drops first (confirmed ones published as departures), then sightings toward confirmation (3 observations, 0.3 s after the first), forgetting unconfirmed tracks no longer held; `clear()` lets everyone leave as `"stopped"`.
3. `FaceDetection(users=...)`: `_observe_users` after each observation (`set` on a change or the first active report, `update` otherwise); `_new_tracks(active=...)` at start, at stop, on a swap and on a detector down.
4. `_publish`: `set` on a new track id at once, on a missing one after `FACE_ABSENT_S`.
5. The bridge's `users`, its reset at exit, the exports.
6. Tests, specs, docs.
