# Tracks anchored in the world: a face the robot's own move takes out of view is not gone

**Status:** Done

Implements the settled behaviour in `specs/vision/user_perception.md` ("Tracks": the direction, the expected pixel, the in-view rule, `UNOBSERVABLE_MAX_S`) and `specs/motion/head_tracking.md` ("Whom the head follows", "Easing, loss, focus": the clocks counting only the observations that could have shown the face). It delivers: the detection loop's tracks matched where the head's own motion predicts them and kept through the time the camera does not frame them; the head tracker neither switching nor losing while its face is out of the camera's view; the same `track_id` and `attention` across an emotion that turns the head away. It deliberately leaves out: an emotion played in the aim's frame (a design of its own), and re-identifying a person after a gap longer than the net.

## Scope

- `src/reachy_mini_bridge/head_tracking.py` — `expected_pixel(direction, head_pose, camera) -> (u, v) | None`: the forward projection of a world direction through the frame's head pose, the head-to-camera transform and `K` / `D` (the closed-form distortion of upstream's model, up to 12 coefficients); `world_direction(u, v, head_pose, camera)`: the inverse, the pixel undistorted into a ray and rotated into the world; `frame_head_pose` taking `(ts, head_pose)` in place of a report (the report's two fields, so the detection loop can call it before a report exists; the tracker and the face markers pass the report's); the `TrackGeometry` protocol; the tracker's in-view rule on `observe` and `tick` — `_missing_for` returning the time since the last sighting **less** the out-of-view time accumulated since it, capped by `UNOBSERVABLE_MAX_S = 20.0`; `in_frame(pixel, size)` shared with the loop — no margin: a departing person's last sighting lies inside the frame and must count.
- `src/reachy_mini_bridge/face_detection.py` — `_Track.direction`; `_FaceTracks(geometry=None)`: the expected pixel per track at each `update`, matching against it, misses counted for tracks in view only, the drop at `UNOBSERVABLE_MAX_S` since the last sighting; the loop handing `update` the frame (its `ts` and `head_pose`) so the geometry can place it; a loop without geometry unchanged.
- `src/reachy_mini_bridge/bridge.py` — the bridge builds one `TrackGeometry` from the session's camera model and the tracker's frame-pose rule (its `delay_s`) and hands it to the detection loop; nothing else wired.
- `src/reachy_mini_bridge/sim_displays.py` — the face marker publisher's `frame_head_pose` call on the new signature.
- `tests/test_head_tracking.py` — `expected_pixel` the inverse of the tracker's aim geometry (a pixel → direction → pixel round trip through the Lite's `K` / `D` and the sim's pinhole, from a turned head); the `_Chooser` driven with a geometry stub flipping in-view / out of view, pinning the two clocks.
- `tests/test_face_detection.py` — `_FaceTracks` with a scripted geometry: a face continuing its track from a predicted pixel after a turn, an out-of-view track keeping its misses, dropped at the net.
- `tests/test_bridge.py` — a **projecting** stub detector (the face at a fixed world direction, drawn where the fake's head pose frames it, absent otherwise — `tests/test_head_tracking.py`'s `_project` lifted into the bridge-level `_Scene`); the four scenarios of the specs' fake support across an emotion on the fake.
- `tests-e2e/test_head_tracking.py` — a live test: `downcast1` (or `sad1`) over a portrait 15° above the axis on the sim, the `track_id` and `attention` held across the move, the head back on the portrait after it.
- `examples/greeter/app.py` — the temporary keyboard addition removed before the commit (it is the shoot's, not the example's).
- `docs/reference/api.md`, `docs/guides/perception-and-tracking.md` — already carry the new sentences (editorial, with the specs).

## Steps

1. **The projection.** In `head_tracking.py`, `expected_pixel`: the direction into the camera frame (`inv(head_pose @ head_to_camera)`), `None` when its z is not positive, the distortion forward (radial `k1..k6` as the rational model, tangential `p1, p2`, thin prism `s1..s4`), then `K`. `world_direction` as the inverse through `undistort_points`. Pin the round trip and the agreement with `look_at_image_pose`'s ray on the Lite's calibration and the sim pinhole, from several head poses. `in_view` with the margin in face sizes.
2. **`frame_head_pose(ts, head_pose, camera, history, delay, now)`.** Change the signature, update the three callers (the tracker's `_aim`, the markers, the tests). The `TrackGeometry` protocol and a `_BridgeTrackGeometry` built from a camera model and a frame-pose callable.
3. **The tracks.** `_Track` gains `direction: ndarray | None`; `_FaceTracks.update(faces, frame)` computes each track's expected pixel through the geometry (the last centre without one or without a direction), matches against it, sets the matched track's direction from its new centre, counts misses for in-view tracks only, drops at `TRACK_MAX_MISSES` in-view misses or at `UNOBSERVABLE_MAX_S` since the last sighting (`seen_at` on the track, the frame's `ts`). Pin with a scripted geometry.
4. **The tracker's clocks.** `HeadTracker` takes the geometry too (or builds the in-view test from its own camera and the frame pose it already computes): on `observe`, the followed face is in view when `expected_pixel` of the aim's look direction, through this report's frame pose, is inside the frame by the margin of its last size; on `tick`, through the history's latest pose. Out of view: accumulate the poll's elapsed time into `_unobservable_s` (capped at `UNOBSERVABLE_MAX_S`); `_missing_for = now − seen_at − _unobservable_s`; a sighting resets both. The switch and the loss read the same value. Pin on the `_Chooser` with the geometry stub.
5. **The bridge.** Build the geometry once per session beside the camera model and hand it to `FaceDetection`; the tracker gets it the same way.
6. **The fake scenarios.** The projecting `_Scene` in `tests/test_bridge.py`; the emotion-across-a-face tests of both specs' fake support (the fake's `sad` nod is 0.3 s — give the scene a long custom move or a scripted stub whose "move" hides the face by projection for longer than `TRACKING_LOST_S`: the fake's head is wherever the loop sends it, so a `set_idle_move` sweeping the head 60° away for 3 s is a primary-free way to take the face out of view; an emotion through a stub library move of 3 s is the other).
7. **Live.** The e2e test on the sim. The Lite with a person — the goodbye moves and the long ones at a standing person, the log read for track drops, the blur question — is head_tracking.md's open question 3, read after the merge; it measures the rule, it does not gate the code matching the spec.
8. **Close.** The greeter's keyboard addition out, `config.example.json` back to `sim`; the specs to `Implemented`, this plan `Done`; `analysis/emotion-while-tracking.md` and its probe folder (untracked) deleted once the Lite reading is in.
