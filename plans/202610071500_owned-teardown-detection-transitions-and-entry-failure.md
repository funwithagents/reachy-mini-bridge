# The teardown owned past a cancel, the detection transitions one at a time and owned, a primary's entry failure propagated

**Status:** Done

Implements the settled behaviour of [bridge.md](../specs/core/bridge.md) "Lifecycle" (a cancelled `stop()` completes: the motion thread never outlives the session) and "Cancellation" (one mode transition at a time, the last call's value standing; an accepted disable completes), [motion.md](../specs/motion/motion.md) "Lifecycle" (a primary's entry failure fails its future) and [user_perception.md](../specs/vision/user_perception.md) "Lifecycle" (the loop's `stop()` owned). Closes implementation findings 1–4 of the [consistency review of 2026-10-07](../analysis/20261007_project-consistency-and-documentation.md), step 1 of its recommended sequence; finding 5 (a factory's `ValueError` raised raw) and the documentation items are its step 2, left out here. ## Steps

1. **Finding 1 — `stop()` owned.** `stop()` awaited `stack.aclose()` bare while the bring-up failure path already unwound through `_owned`. A cancel landing during the motion session's exit blend cut the `asyncio.to_thread` join short; the exit stack went on to the next callbacks — the detection loop, wobbling, the camera, the media session, the robot's `__exit__` — while the motion thread finished its blend and kept sending targets (nine of them in the next 150 ms on the fake). The unwind now runs through `owned`, the mode reset in the `finally` runs as before, and the absorbed cancel is re-raised once everything is down. The helper moves to `concurrency.py` so the detection loop (step 4) shares it.

2. **Finding 2 — a primary's entry failure.** `_select_next` popped the primary from the queue before `_tick` read the present pose and built the entry blend (`blend_into` evaluates the move at 0); an exception there reached `_run`'s handler with `_playing` still `None`, so `_fail_current` found nothing to fail and the primary was in neither the queue nor the playing slot — its `play_emotion` pending for good, the wobbling lease held with it. The entry's fallible work is now wrapped: a lost-connection error hands the primary back to the head of the queue, which `_on_lost_connection` then fails with every queued one and the chained `BridgeError`; any other error fails that primary's future at once (`_fail_primary`) and is re-raised for the loop's one-warning handling, after which the loop re-selects on its next tick.

3. **Finding 3 — the detection switch under its own lock.** `set_face_detection` flipped `_face_detection_wanted` and rolled it back outside `_detection_sync`, which serialises only the loop's start / stop decision: a first enable whose build failed rolled the switch back to its own `was_wanted` after a second enable had already set it, and the second call's locked sync then read the switch off, did nothing, and returned success — the switch off and no loop running, against "one at a time per mode; the last call's value stands". The verb now holds `_face_detection_sync` (per session, like `_tracking_sync`) over the whole transition, so a call's rollback can only ever undo its own change and the next call runs on the state the previous one left.

4. **Finding 4 — the loop's `stop()` owned.** `FaceDetection.stop()` cancelled and awaited its task, then released the detector on a worker (`close()` waiting on the detector lock a `detect` in flight holds); a cancel of the caller during that release left the loop stopped with the detector unreleased, and the verb's rollback then set the switch back on — a switch on, a report inactive, no loop. The body is now owned: the cancel is absorbed until the release has run and propagates then, and the verb does not roll back a cancelled disable (the stop has completed). The `with suppress(CancelledError): await task` that awaited the task's end also swallowed a caller's cancel landing there; `await asyncio.wait({task})` reads the task's own cancellation as its outcome and leaves the caller's cancel to the owner.

5. **Tests, each observed failing on the previous code.** The teardown one cancels `stop()` inside the exit blend and asserts the `CancelledError` propagates with the last target at neutral and no target sent afterwards, the robot exited, and the bridge starting again. The entry-failure ones: on the bridge, a recorded move whose `evaluate` raises makes `play_emotion` raise within the second with wobbling re-enabled and the next emotion playing; on the session, the primary's future fails and the idle goes on, and a present-pose read raising a lost connection fails both queued primaries with the chained `BridgeError`. The detection ones: two enables with the first build failing leave the switch on, the second detector running and two builds; a disable cancelled while its release waits on a blocked `detect` raises `CancelledError` only once the detector is closed, with the switch off and the report inactive, and the next enable runs a fresh detector.
