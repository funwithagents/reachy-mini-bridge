## Steps

1. **Dependencies.** Add `opentelemetry-api` to `[project.dependencies]` and `opentelemetry-sdk` to the `dev` group; `uv sync --dev`.
2. **`tracing.py`.** The name constants and `Outcome`. `VerbSpan`: `time.time_ns()` and `time.perf_counter_ns()` read at construction, the span started with that start time in the current context; `now()` and `at(ts)` on that anchor; `mark(name, at=None)` once per name under a lock and a no-op after the end; `__enter__` attaching the span to the OpenTelemetry context and to a `ContextVar` of the module, `__exit__` detaching both and ending with the outcome the exception gives (`SpeechInterruptedError` / `SoundInterruptedError` → `interrupted`, `CancelledError` → `cancelled`, any other → `failed` with `record_exception` and the `ERROR` status). `current()`, `mark()`, `set_attribute()` over the `ContextVar`. `wall_clock(ts)` from `time.time()` and `time.perf_counter()` read at the call.
3. **The audio session.** `say` sets `streamed` false; `say_stream` wraps the text stream in `_FirstTextMarked` — a class, holding the span current at its construction, closing its source when closed, so the text stream is closed on every path as before and the stamp lands whichever task the synthesizer pulls the text from — and sets `streamed`. `_stream_to_speaker` marks `first_push` on the first non-empty push, sets `audio_seconds` when the stream ends on any path, and marks `playback_ended` at the tracker's end once the wait for it completed. `play_sound` sets `audio_seconds` to the file's duration and marks `sound_started` when the start step returns.
4. **The motion loop.** `_Primary` gains `motion_started_at`, `trajectory_started_at`, `trajectory_ended_at`; `_tick` writes the tick's `now` where it makes a primary the playing one, where its stage becomes the trajectory, and where it resolves the primary's future.
5. **The bridge.** Each of the seven verbs runs inside `tracing.verb_span(...)` with its attributes; `play_emotion` and `set_motors_state` keep their bodies in a private method the span wraps. `play_emotion` marks `queued` after `motion.submit` and, in its `finally`, places the primary's three times as events (`at=`), after the cancel path has waited for the loop's acknowledgement.
6. **`User.first_seen_ts`.** `_Users` keeps the candidate's first sighting when it confirms a track, drops it with the track and on `clear`.
7. **Tests** (`tests/test_tracing.py`), each on the `fake` with the SDK's `InMemorySpanExporter` behind a provider installed once per process: one `say` span under the caller's, `first_push` after the synthesizer's silence and `playback_ended` the audio's duration later; `say_stream`'s `first_text` and `streamed`, through a streaming and a whole-text synthesizer; a synthesizer's own span, opened in a task it creates, a child of the `say` span; an interrupted, a cancelled and a failed `say`; an emotion's four events in order with the blend and the trajectory between them; a second emotion's `motion_started` at the first's `trajectory_ended`; an emotion cancelled in the queue and in its blend; `play_sound` started, replaced, cancelled, missing; `set_motors_state` and the head-tracking verbs; no span for a verb that reads state; one span per call with the caller's span current again; an event stamped once and never after the end; `wall_clock`. The two tests whose second call must land while the first plays carry `realtime`.
8. **Specs and docs.** `specs/core/tracing.md`, its index row and the overview's principle; the sentences in the five specs; the project map row; the API reference's "Tracing" section.
