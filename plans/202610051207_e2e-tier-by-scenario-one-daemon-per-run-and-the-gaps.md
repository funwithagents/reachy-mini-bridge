- **the redundancy removed**: `test_say_pipeline_runs_to_the_speaker` deleted (contained in the completion test); the capabilities probed once per run instead of once per module (headless burned the camera probe's full 5 s on every module);
- **the spawned daemon listens on the port it was asked for**: `launch_command` passes `--fastapi-host` / `--fastapi-port`, so `REACHY_MINI_PORT` and a config's `robot.port` move a *spawned* daemon too, not only a borrowed one.
