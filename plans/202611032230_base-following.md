# The body yaw: the base toward the person

**Status:** In progress

**Steps 1–8 done (2026-11-03):** config, the loop (the frame, the goal and the ease, reach with the base, the exit), the bridge verb and properties, the panel, the docs, the fake tests, the live kit judged relative to the base and the viewer-sim test — the static gate, the fast tier and the live tier on the viewer sim green. **Step 9 in progress:** a first walk on the Lite (2026-11-03) found the base turning well but reading as the body turning rather than the head; a lag of 0.8 s before a turn and a slower ease (two stages of 0.6 s, the exit 4 s) answer it — the beat and the pace to be judged on the Lite again, then the default.

## Steps

1. **Config.** `BaseFollowingSettings`, `MotionSettings.base_following`, parsing and bounds, `config.example.json` and the configuration reference (`tests/test_docs_consistency.py` wants both). Tests: defaults, a partial object, each bound, the boolean's error.
2. **The loop, the frame.** Keep the stages in the move frame and apply `Rz(θ)` at the emit point; remember the last target as sent; bring a blend's source — the last target, or the pose read from the robot with `θ` set to its body yaw — into the move frame. Pin on the fake with `θ` pinned by hand (a test seam, before the goal exists): an emotion's targets `Rz(θ) ·` the recording with body yaw `θ` throughout, no swing at either blend; a resume on a robot whose base is turned adopting it; the first tick of every blend emitting exactly the last target.
3. **The goal and the ease.** The turn through the deadband, the hold, the return, the switch; the two-stage filter stepped by the tick's elapsed time, pure and pinned on its own (at rest at the start, continuous under a moving goal, 90 % in about 1.5 s); `body_yaw` on the session. Pin through the loop with a scripted aim: past the deadband the recorded body yaw eases onto the aim's yaw with no step between two ticks and the head poses rotate with it; inside it nothing moves; the aim kept current during a turn is followed; withdrawn, the body yaw holds past `TRACKING_LOST_S` and eases back after `hold_s` (a short hold in the test); the switch off eases back; a change during an emotion waits for the idle entry.
4. **Reach with the base.** `analytical_reach` and `Reachable` take the body yaw; the direction share shrinks toward `Rz(θ)`; the existing reach tests adapted; a new one: the full retreat with a 25° roll at 60° of yaw, refused on a front-facing base, kept whole once the base has turned.
5. **The exit.** `BODY_YAW_EXIT_S` when the body yaw is off the front, the join timeout with it; pinned: the exit ends within half a degree of the front, body yaw included, and a cancelled emotion leaves the session on the body yaw.
6. **The bridge and the panel.** The verb, the two properties, outside-session values and the reset at exit; the panel's checkbox, state fields and readout; the api reference and the guide. Tests on the fake through the bridge: the switch recorded and applied, `body_yaw_deg` following the loop, `0.0` outside a session; the panel's snapshot.
7. **The fake tests that assumed a front-facing base.** Audit `tests/test_head_tracking.py` and `tests/test_bridge.py` for assertions on the head's world yaw after a loss or on neutral: judge relative to the base, or run with the body yaw off where the base is not the subject.
8. **The live tier.** The convergence kit relative to the base; `tests-e2e/test_head_tracking.py`: a portrait spawned far to one side (past the deadband) turns the base joint onto it within a generous window (the sim's servos are slow), the head centred on the face; the portrait hidden, the base stays (within the deadband) past the loss while the head rests on the body yaw; the existing tracking tests pass with the body yaw on. Run the viewer sim (`REACHY_MINI_E2E_SIM_VIEWER=1`), then headless on both CI engines.
9. **The Lite with a person.** Deadband, ease, hold, exit, and whether the default stays on; record the outcome in `motion.md` open question 8 and adjust the constants or the defaults. Then: the four specs back to `Implemented` (here and in `specs/_index.md`), this plan `Done`, `analysis/comfort_body yaw_analysis.md` deleted with anything left open moved into `analysis/global_analysis.md`.
