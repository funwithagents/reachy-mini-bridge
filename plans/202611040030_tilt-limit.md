# The tilt limit: a head that looks up and keeps turning

**Status:** In progress

**Steps 1–3 done (2026-11-04):** the field, the clamp in the gaze layer, the tests and the docs — the static gate and the fast tier green. **Step 4 open:** the Lite with a person standing.

Implements `specs/motion/motion.md` ("The gaze layer": "The tilt limit"; open question 9) and `specs/core/config.md` (`motion.max_tilt_deg`). Delivers the gaze layer holding every pose it composes to a tilt limit, the yaw kept, so a robot looking up at a standing person keeps turning toward them instead of stalling against its mechanical ceiling. Leaves out: a runtime verb for the limit, a limit on primaries (recordings play as recorded), and a separate fix of the frame-delay estimate, which is re-measured once the head no longer stalls.

## Scope

- `src/reachy_mini_bridge/config.py` — `MotionSettings.max_tilt_deg` (default 25, in `(0, 35]`), `MAX_TILT_CEILING_DEG`
- `src/reachy_mini_bridge/motion.py` — `tilt_of`, `within_tilt` (the least rotation back to the limit, then the yaw restored about the vertical), applied to every candidate the gaze layer's reach search composes; `MotionSession(max_tilt_deg=…)`
- `src/reachy_mini_bridge/bridge.py` — the config's value handed to the session
- `config.example.json`, `docs/reference/configuration.md`, `docs/guides/perception-and-tracking.md` — the field and a sentence on a person standing above the robot
- `tests/test_motion.py`, `tests/test_config.py` — the clamp on its own (the limit reached exactly, the yaw and the translation kept, a pose within it untouched), the loop holding a raised aim at the limit with its yaw, the field's default and bounds

## Steps

1. The field and its bounds; the configuration reference and the example config.
2. `within_tilt` and its tests: the least rotation alone shifts a rolled pose's yaw (1.4° at 45° of yaw with a 10° roll; about 2° on upstream's look-at, which carries a roll for a face up and to the side), so the yaw is restored about the vertical, which tilt does not depend on.
3. The clamp in the gaze layer's composition, before the reach check; the loop test with a look-at 42° up at 20° of yaw, at limits of 25° and 18°.
4. **The Lite with a person standing** (the same live log as the investigation: commanded against reported pose, joint gaps, the delay estimate): the head reaches what it is sent; the yaw follows a sideways step; whether 25° looks up far enough; the delay estimate back near the sim's 0.05–0.25 s or not — if not, its own plan. Then `motion.md` open question 9 updated, the specs' statuses with the base-following plan's, this plan `Done`.
