# `say_stream`: text spoken as it is produced

**Status:** Done

Implements `specs/audio/audio.md` ("TTS out": the two synthesizer protocols, "`say_stream`", "One `say` at a time") and `specs/core/bridge.md` (the `say_stream` verb, the spanning verbs, the front door). Delivers `ReachyMiniBridge.say_stream(text_stream, synth=None)` over `MediaSession.say_stream`, the `StreamingSpeechSynthesizer` protocol, and the rename of the synthesizer seam's `stream(text)` to `synthesize(text)` — an API break, every synthesizer in the repo moved with it. Leaves out sync iterables, sentence segmentation in the bridge, and any report of how much of a cancelled utterance was spoken.

## Scope

- `src/reachy_mini_bridge/audio.py` — `SpeechSynthesizer.synthesize`; `StreamingSpeechSynthesizer`; `MediaSession.say_stream`; `say` / `say_stream` sharing one utterance path over a chunk source; the text stream's close; the whole-text fallback; `TTSEngineSynthesizer.synthesize` / `synthesize_stream` sharing one queue helper
- `src/reachy_mini_bridge/bridge.py` — `ReachyMiniBridge.say_stream`; the synthesizer resolution shared with `say`
- `src/reachy_mini_bridge/__init__.py` — export `StreamingSpeechSynthesizer`
- `tests/test_audio.py`, `tests/test_bridge.py`, `tests/test_control_panel.py`, `tests-e2e/test_audio.py` — the fakes renamed; new functional tests (below)
- `examples/voice_tour/app.py` — its wrapping synthesizer renamed
- `docs/getting-started.md`, `docs/guides/audio.md`, `docs/reference/api.md`, `README.md` — the rename and the new verb

## Steps

1. **Seam.** Rename `SpeechSynthesizer.stream` to `synthesize`; add `StreamingSpeechSynthesizer(SpeechSynthesizer, Protocol)` with `synthesize_stream(text_stream: AsyncIterator[str]) -> AsyncIterator[ndarray]`, `runtime_checkable`, in `__all__`.
2. **Session.** `_speak` / `_stream_to_speaker` take a factory of the chunk iterator and the synth rate instead of the text; `say` passes `lambda: synth.synthesize(text)`. The shared `_utter(source, rate)` holds the exclusivity and the flush. `say_stream`: refuse a `str` (`TypeError`) or a non-async-iterable; require the open session; take the text iterator; with a `StreamingSpeechSynthesizer`, chunks from `synthesize_stream(text_iter)`; otherwise log once per synthesizer (a weak set of ids is enough: `id` of the synthesizer in a set on the session) and use an async generator that joins the text then yields from `synthesize(joined)`. In a `finally`, close the text iterator after the utterance (its `aclose` if any; logged, not raised, when the utterance already failed).
3. **Adapter.** Factor the queue/sink plumbing of `TTSEngineSynthesizer.stream` into `_run(producer)` taking a coroutine factory; `synthesize(text)` runs `engine.say(text)`, `synthesize_stream(text_stream)` runs `engine.say_stream(text_stream)`.
4. **Bridge.** `_resolve_synthesizer(verb, synth)` used by both; `say_stream` calls `MediaSession.say_stream`.
5. **Rename everywhere.** The test fakes, the voice tour, the control panel test, the e2e tone synth, the docs.
6. **Tests (fast tier, on the fake):**
   - audio is pushed before the text stream ends (a stream gated on an event; a streaming fake synth yielding a tone per piece);
   - completes when heard (real-time lower bound) and the text is concatenated verbatim, empty pieces skipped;
   - cancel mid-flight: speaker flushed, text generator's `finally` ran, the session then says again;
   - `say` interrupts `say_stream` and the reverse (`SpeechInterruptedError`), text stream closed;
   - a text stream raising: the same exception, speaker flushed;
   - empty stream: returns at once, nothing pushed;
   - `str` refused with `TypeError`; a list refused;
   - a plain `SpeechSynthesizer`: nothing pushed before the stream ends, the whole text synthesized once, one log line across two calls;
   - the adapter over tts-engine's `tone` module streams through `say_stream`;
   - bridge: `say_stream` without a synthesizer raises `BridgeError`; uses the configured default; exported protocol checks.
7. **e2e.** One streamed utterance on the local phonon model beside the real-TTS test.
8. **Specs** to `Implemented` (status lines and index rows), this plan `Done`.
