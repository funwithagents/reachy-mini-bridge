# Bundled assets

The portraits of the test scene's pool (specs/testing/sim_scene.md "A pool of portraits"),
one person each, in pool order. Each is a crop of a photo on Wikimedia Commons, resized
to 320x400 with the face framed the same way (the face box about half the height). All four
are works of NASA, a U.S. federal government agency: **public domain**.

| File | Prop | Pose | Source | Author |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `armstrong.png` | `face_1` | frontal | NASA's official Apollo 11 portrait of Neil Armstrong (NASA photo S69-31741, 1969), [`File:Neil_Armstrong_pose.jpg`](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Neil_Armstrong_pose.jpg) | NASA |
| `chawla.png` | `face_2` | frontal | [Kalpana Chawla, NASA photo portrait in orange suit](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kalpana_Chawla,_NASA_photo_portrait_in_orange_suit.jpg) (NASA JSC2002e2532) | NASA |
| `schmid.png` | `face_3` | head turned ~30° toward the image's right | [Profiles in Leadership: Joe Schmid is loving life as a NASA flight surgeon and Air Force Reservist](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Profiles_in_Leadership-_Joe_Schmid_is_loving_life_as_a_NASA_flight_surgeon_and_Air_Force_Reservist_150519-F-GS664-033.jpg) (150519-F-GS664-033) | Bill Ingalls, NASA |
| `malenchenko.png` | `face_4` | head tilted down | [View of STS-106 MS cosmonaut Malenchenko suiting up for an EVA](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:View_of_STS-106_MS_cosmonaut_Malenchenko_suiting_up_for_an_EVA.jpg) (NASA jsc2000e23502) | NASA |
