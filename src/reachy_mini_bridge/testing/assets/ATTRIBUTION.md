# Bundled assets

- `face.png` — head-and-shoulders crop of NASA's official Apollo 11 portrait of Neil
  Armstrong (NASA photo S69-31741, 1969), via Wikimedia Commons
  (`File:Neil_Armstrong_pose.jpg`). A work of the U.S. federal government: **public
  domain**. Used as the default portrait `reachy_mini_bridge.testing.sim_scene.write_test_scene`
  textures onto the sim's face plane (specs/sim_scene.md), because it is a frontal,
  well-lit face the upstream YuNet detector picks up reliably once rendered.
